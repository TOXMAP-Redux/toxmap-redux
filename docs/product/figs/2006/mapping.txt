PMC2703818 is the article ID for the 2006 Article (Roth SL, Med Ref Serv Q 2006): 12 figures of original TOXMAP (ArcIMS era).

The rule is to replace the {N} in the URL with the figure number you want to access.

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2703818/figure/F{N}/

maps to local file: nihms116260f{N}.jpg

For instance, if you want to access figure 1, you would use:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2703818/figure/F1/
